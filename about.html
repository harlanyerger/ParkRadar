What it is
ParkRadar is a free app from an independent startup. It helps people find parking near the University of Mississippi. It is not run by, endorsed by, or affiliated with the university.

Email us to ask a question, report a wrong or abusive report, ask for something to be corrected or removed, or make a request about your data.

Deleting your data

  • In the app. Open the Me tab and tap Delete my data. It removes the reports and "how full" updates you posted, the history of those updates, your settings and saved car, and your anonymous account, and then clears what ParkRadar saved on your device.
  • By email. If that does not work, or you no longer have the app, email us. ParkRadar does not know who you are, so tell us roughly what you posted and when. We will do what we can.
  • What stays. A "still there" or "it's gone" tap on someone else's report is stored only with an anonymous ID, and it disappears with that report. See the Privacy Policy.
