Refund Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026. This applies to the ParkRadar iPhone app and the ParkRadar website. It goes with the Terms of Service.

The short version

  • ParkRadar is free today, so there is nothing to refund. We do not charge for the app or the website, and they cannot take a payment.
  • Joining the ParkRadar+ waitlist is not a purchase. It takes no payment and asks for no payment details.
  • If paid features launch, this page will be updated with the exact refund terms before anyone can buy. Purchases made through Apple's App Store are refunded by Apple, under Apple's rules.

ParkRadar today

ParkRadar has no purchases, subscriptions or in-app payments. We do not collect card numbers or other payment details. Everything in the app and on the website is free.

If you were ever charged money for ParkRadar, it was not by us. Please email us with what happened, so we can look into it, for example if someone is selling a copy of the app.

ParkRadar+ (planned)

The Me tab shows a planned paid version called ParkRadar+, with a planned price of $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year. That is a plan, not an offer. The price, the features and whether it launches at all can change. Joining the waitlist only tells us you are interested. It is not an order, and it commits neither you nor us to anything.

If paid features launch

  • We will tell you first. Before anything can be bought, we will update this page with the refund terms, and the purchase screen will show the price, how often you are billed, and how to cancel.
  • Purchases through the App Store. Apple takes the payment, so Apple decides and issues the refund. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com or from your purchase history in your Apple Account. We cannot refund App Store purchases ourselves.
  • Cancelling a subscription. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose ParkRadar+. Cancelling stops future renewals, and you keep the paid features until the end of the period you already paid for. Money back for a period you already paid for is decided by Apple under its rules, as described in section 8 of the Terms of Service.
  • Anything sold outside the App Store. Its refund terms will be on this page before you pay.

Mistakes and problems

If you are ever charged by mistake, charged twice, or something you bought does not work, email us and we will help you sort it out, whether that means fixing it or pointing you to the right place to ask for a refund.

Your legal rights

Nothing on this page limits any rights you have under the law, including consumer rights to a refund that cannot be waived.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page, and the date at the top, whenever the way ParkRadar handles payments or refunds changes.
