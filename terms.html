3. The information is crowdsourced and may be wrong

Almost everything in ParkRadar comes from other students and from estimates:

  • Enforcement reports and road closures are posted by people who may be mistaken, may have seen something that has since moved, or may be wrong on purpose. A report, or the lack of one, does not mean enforcement is or is not nearby.
  • How full a lot is is the latest student report from the past hour, or an estimate from typical weekday traffic when nobody has reported.
  • Predictions, such as the chance of finding a spot and the time to leave, are estimates based on past reports and typical traffic. They are not promises, and nothing here guarantees you will find a spot or arrive on time.
  • Permits, zones and rules are shown as listed on the campus parking map and can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Check the university's current parking rules.
  • Maps, routes and walking times are approximate. Positions on the map and from your phone's location can be off by several meters.
  • Reminders and notifications can arrive late or not at all (for example because of silent mode, a Focus setting, low battery or a bug). Do not rely on them as your only way to avoid missing a class or a time limit.

Use your own eyes and judgment. When ParkRadar and the real world disagree, the real world wins.
